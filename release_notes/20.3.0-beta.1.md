## New

- **PyroWave.** The low-latency PyroWave codec now decodes and presents natively with Vulkan. On a Vibepollo host it is offered as an opt-in setting (the switch is visible without expanding Advanced Settings). PyroWave streams get their own bitrate, are no longer asked for HDR, keep the default wavelet precision on Adreno, and show their stats in the overlay and on the companion panel. The renderer now stops before its surface goes away, which fixes a crash when leaving a stream.
- **Reconnect after a network drop.** When the connection to the host goes silent, the stream reconnects on its own instead of ending. The decoder and the last frame stay on screen meanwhile (HDR metadata included), a reconnect attempt can be cancelled for real, and the silence timeout defaults to 5 s, like the host's. The stream no longer blocks the UI while watching the connection, and a stale poor-connection warning from the dead connection is dropped.
- **Adaptive bitrate and live bitrate.** On a Vibepollo host, the in-game menu changes the bitrate of the running stream, and an adaptive controller can raise and lower it by itself: fewer, larger steps up, and a quick drop on heavy loss. The host's limits are respected and the menu says when adaptation stops.
- **Video error correction.** A new setting for dynamic FEC. With dynamic FEC the client leaves parity to the host on live bitrate changes, and whole lost FEC blocks are counted correctly.
- **Smoother frame pacing.** The frame pacing setting is honoured again, "prefer lower delays" drops queued frames in Balanced, frames are no longer dropped by age outside Balanced, a frame is presented without waiting for the next one, and the renderer follows display refresh rate changes. The refresh rate is measured on the mode the stream asked for and sent to the host.
- **Keyboard on the PC's text fields.** When the PC focuses a text field, the Android keyboard opens. Passwords stay out of the keyboard's suggestions, and the in-game menu no longer ends up under it.
- **Companion panel.** Tap the telemetry strip for the full stream stats.

## Notes

- Live bitrate, adaptive bitrate and PyroWave need a Vibepollo host; on a stock host nothing changes.
- The app now ships in English only.
